Privacy Policy

Social Media Evaluator

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Social Media Evaluator is an application designed to help authorized Instagram users analyze account and content performance, generate content ideas and drafts, manage content, publish approved content, and assist with engagement and comment management.

1. Information We Access

When a user connects an Instagram account, Social Media Evaluator may access information made available through Meta's Instagram APIs and authorized by the user.

This may include:

  • Instagram profile and account information
  • Instagram media and captions
  • Engagement metrics and insights
  • Comments and related engagement information
  • Other information necessary to provide features authorized by the user

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed through Instagram is used to provide application functionality, including:

  • Analyzing Instagram content performance
  • Identifying content and engagement patterns
  • Generating content recommendations and drafts
  • Assisting users with captions and content ideas
  • Publishing content when explicitly requested by the user
  • Retrieving and managing comments
  • Generating suggested responses to comments

3. AI Processing

Certain information may be processed using artificial intelligence services to provide analysis, recommendations, content generation, or other AI-assisted features requested by the user.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

Social Media Evaluator may use third-party service providers where necessary to provide application functionality, such as AI processing, media hosting, cloud infrastructure, analytics, or related technical services.

Information is provided to these services only as reasonably necessary to perform the requested application functionality.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the application or when disclosure is required by applicable law.

6. Instagram and Meta

Social Media Evaluator uses Meta's Instagram APIs. Use of Instagram data is subject to applicable Meta platform terms, policies, and user permissions.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide application functionality, satisfy legitimate operational requirements, comply with applicable requirements, or until deletion is requested, subject to applicable legal obligations.

8. Revoking Instagram Access

Users may revoke the application's access to their Instagram account through their Instagram or Meta account settings where applicable.

9. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with their account by contacting us using the email address below.

Valid deletion requests will be processed in accordance with applicable requirements.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information processed by the application. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
