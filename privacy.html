Privacy Policy – Social Media Evaluator Last updated: August 13, 2026 Social Media Evaluator is an application designed to help authorized Instagram users analyze account performance, generate content ideas, manage content, publish approved content, and assist with comment management. Information We Access When a user connects an Instagram account, the application may access information authorized through Meta's Instagram API, including Instagram profile information, media, captions, engagement metrics and insights, comments, and other information necessary to provide features authorized by the user. How We Use Information Information is used to provide Instagram analytics, identify content-performance patterns, generate content recommendations and drafts, publish content when explicitly requested, retrieve comments, and generate suggested responses. Some authorized information may be processed by third-party service providers used to provide application functionality, including AI and media-hosting services. Data Sharing We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with service providers only when necessary to provide application functionality or when required by law. Instagram and Meta The application uses Meta's Instagram APIs and is subject to applicable Meta platform terms and policies. Data Retention Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's functionality, comply with applicable requirements, or until deletion is requested, subject to applicable legal requirements. Revoking Access Users may revoke the application's access to their Instagram account through their Instagram/Meta account settings where applicable.
